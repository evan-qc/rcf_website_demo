Flooring

Find the right floor for every room.

The best way to choose a floor is to see it in person. Stop in and we'll help you compare looks, feel and durability side by side, and send you home with samples.

Carpet

Nothing beats carpet for warmth on a January morning. We carry a wide range of colors, textures and price points for bedrooms, family rooms, basements and stairs, plus carpet remnants in stock for smaller rooms and quick projects.

  • Bedrooms
  • Living rooms
  • Stairs
  • Remnants in stock

Luxury vinyl & hybrid

Luxury vinyl plank (LVP) and tile (LVT) give you the look of wood or stone with a waterproof core, a big plus for kitchens, baths, mudrooms and camps. New this season: ReNue Hybrid, which pairs the comfort of LVP with the strength of laminate.

  • Waterproof
  • Pet friendly
  • Kitchens & baths
  • Camps & rentals

Hardwood

Real wood adds warmth and value that lasts for decades. We stock hardwood and can order a wide range of species, stains and plank widths to match the character of your home.

  • Living & dining rooms
  • Adds resale value
  • In stock & special order

Laminate

Laminate delivers a convincing wood look with a hard, scratch-resistant surface at a friendly price. It's a smart choice for busy hallways, kids' rooms and anywhere you want style without the worry.

  • Scratch resistant
  • Budget friendly
  • High-traffic areas

Ceramic & porcelain tile

Tile is tough, timeless and easy to keep clean. Choose from classic ceramics and dense porcelain in stone, wood and modern looks for floors, entryways and bathrooms.

  • Bathrooms
  • Entryways
  • Kitchens

Commercial flooring

From offices to schools and storefronts, we supply and install commercial carpet and commercial vinyl made to handle heavy foot traffic for years.

  • Commercial carpet
  • Commercial vinyl
  • Offices & schools
