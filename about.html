About us

A family business, built for our community.

Roggies' Flooring Center started with a simple idea: give Lowville a flooring store where you're treated like a neighbor and get the same great products as the big cities.

Our story

From a downtown storefront to the Number 4 Road.

In the fall of 2005, Will Roggie and his wife Dawn started talking about a flooring business that would serve the community they loved. Their best idea was to ask Ben and LouAnn Roggie to join the adventure, and in November 2005 they said yes.

Two decades later we're still family owned, still local, and still here to help you find a floor you'll love.

  1. 2005

    The idea

    Will and Dawn team up with Ben and LouAnn Roggie.

  2. 2006

    Doors open

    Our first 1,800 sq. ft. showroom opens in the historic Bateman Hotel in downtown Lowville.

  3. 2008

    Room to grow

    Within two years, a warehouse is added so we can keep more flooring in stock.

  4. 2011

    Our own building

    In December we move into a new 4,500 sq. ft. home on the Number 4 Road, with a bigger showroom, ample parking and more warehouse space.

  5. Today

    A new chapter

    After many years of installing, Ben has retired. We miss Ben and LouAnn and wish them all the best.

“It is our desire to create an atmosphere that is professional yet has the hometown flavor, where you as a customer can have a personal shopping experience that is enjoyable.”

Will Roggie, owner

The Flooring Network

Local store, regional buying power.

We're a member of The Flooring Network, the largest flooring buying group in the Northeast. That membership gives a hometown store like ours access to the latest styles and technology, at real value.

Pair that with a warehouse full of stock and our own installers, and you get big-store selection with small-town service.
